The Foundry Process

Zero to launch,
in four heats.

A foundry doesn't admire raw material — it works it. Every venture moves through the same four heats, each with a hard deliverable at the end.

  1. Heat 01

    Cast — shape the thesis

    We pressure-test the idea against the market, define the wedge, and scope a first version that's worth funding — not a demo, a business.

    Deliverable — thesis memo & build spec
  2. Heat 02

    Forge — build with elite talent

    A hand-picked pod of engineers from NYU, Cornell, and Brown builds alongside our operators. Weekly demos, working software — no black boxes, no hand-offs.

    Deliverable — working MVP in users' hands
  3. Heat 03

    Temper — harden against reality

    Real users, real evals, real numbers. We iterate on the metal until retention and unit economics hold under stress — then we cut what doesn't.

    Deliverable — traction the data room can defend
  4. Heat 04

    Launch — ship, then raise

    Public launch with a seed-ready package: the deck, the data room, the metrics, and warm introductions to investors who back companies like yours.

    Deliverable — launched company, open round

The Talent Network

The sharpest students in the country, on your build.

We recruit engineers, researchers, and designers from NYU, Cornell, Brown, and Ivy League programs — then hold them to a production bar, not a classroom one.

  • AEarned, not enrolled. Every builder enters through a paid trial build reviewed by our operators — talent earns its place in the network.
  • BPods, not freelancers. Teams of three to five, led by a senior operator. Never handed off, never outsourced.
  • CProduction bar. Weekly reviews against shipping standards — evals, latency, cost — not demo-day standards.
